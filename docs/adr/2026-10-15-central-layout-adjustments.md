# Central "Clientes": the owner's layout adjustments of 15/10/2026 (rail scroll, Dia from 00h, pace colors, peak map, Financeiro by situation, history picker, faster tabs)
- Date: 2026-10-15
- Status: Accepted (owner decisions of 15/10/2026 after seeing deliveries 1 and 2; awaiting architect review)
- Area: ui, admin, data

## Context
The owner used the central on his own machine and listed fifteen points: the page scrolled below the clients card, the "Dia" chart started at "the hour after yesterday's", columns above the pace line looked like the others, the heatmap was hard to read, Financeiro showed fields that do not apply ("Fim do teste" and "Carencia ate" read as the same thing, "Provedor: Nenhum"), a "Feito" line after actions, seven situation chips, a usage meter with no visible track, a donut number drawn over its tooltip, a back button that highlighted the whole row, a header that hid account type and plan, a flat Visao geral, no way to see past months, and a detail that opened and switched tabs slowly. Cap per plan was left out on purpose (the owner is defining the numbers).

## Decision
- Rail scroll: the whole page scrolled because `NavRail` is taller than the viewport at about 660px or less (647px of content, more with the owner's extra "Clientes" item), and the shell is `h-dvh`. The rail items now live in an `AreaRolavel` (`data-slot="rail-itens"`) between the fixed brand and the fixed footer. Every screen benefits, not only the central. Measured at 1366x650: document height 659 before, 650 after (equal to the window), same at 600 and 560.
- Second cause of the same complaint (owner's screenshot at about 1360x775: an empty strip under the card and a page scrollbar next to the card's own): the text table of every chart (`ChartTabela`, `sr-only`, which is `position: absolute`) had the PAGE as containing block, because no ancestor was positioned. It sat at the height it has inside the scrolled content and stretched the document (1046px of `scrollHeight` in a 775px window, measured up the tree: only `html` overflowed). `ChartTabela` now wraps it in a `relative` div, so the table belongs to the scroll area. Proven at 1360x775 and 1440x900 on the list and on every tab (document height equals the window, the card ends 12px above the bottom).
- Dia from 00h: `chavesDoDiaCivil` (`lib/metrics.ts`) is the civil day of Sao Paulo up to the current hour (15 columns at 14h). `chavesDoEsqueleto` (the panel's rolling 24 hours) is untouched, so `/painel` and the Atendimento tab behave as before. The Uso tab's tiles follow the same chip, so "Dia" there means "hoje, desde as 00h" (legend text); the hourly read window (25 hours) already covers it.
- Pace colors: columns strictly above the pace line are a second stacked series (`barrasComRitmo`: `abaixo` and `acima`, one always zero) painted with `--chart-acima` (an alias of `--warn-fill`, state, not a series). The legend adds "Acima do ritmo" when some column passes, the tooltip adds "Acima do ritmo +N%", the text table has a column. The line already derives from the company's cap (`ritmoDoLimite`); changing the cap moves it (spec kept).
- Peak map: 7 days (Monday first, the work week) x 8 bands of 3 hours instead of 7 x 24 squares of 20px. Cells are 36px tall with the number written inside (empty cells blank), one-hue ramp, text ink on the light steps and white on the full brand step (contrast: ink on step 3 is 5.2:1 after moving it from 70% to 62%; white on step 4 is 7.0:1 light and 5.8:1 dark). The peak is outlined and said in text twice: the busiest band and the busiest hour inside it. The hourly grid (`gradeDeHoras`) stays the data; `gradeEmFaixas` sums it, so no new SQL.
- Financeiro by situation: `linhasDaAssinatura(conta, kind)` (`lib/admin-conta.ts`) picks the date rows from `accessState().kind`: "O teste termina em" only in trial ("terminou em" after), "Carencia do pagamento ate" only with late payment ("terminou em" when blocked), "Vitalicio desde" and "Bloqueado desde" only when the mark exists. The provider card exists only when `provedor` is set, and shows only the ids that exist; with no provider the Cobrancas card carries ONE line: "Sem cobranca: conta vitalicia." for a vitalicio company, otherwise "Sem assinatura no Asaas." (`textoSemAssinatura`). No value is invented.
- No "Feito" text: a successful action updates badge, buttons and dates, so nothing is written. The preview note ("Preview: nada foi gravado.") and the "recarregue" note (write committed, re-read failed) stay.
- Situation filter: one icon button next to the search (`DropdownMenu`, "Todos" plus the seven situations, a check on the active one, a "1" badge on the button while a filter is active). The list keeps the same server filter.
- Visual fixes in the base layer: `Medidor` track is `line-strong` (it was `bloco`, which is the surface of a `secao` card and nearly the `raised` one in light; `neutro` fill moved to `ink-3` so it differs from the track); the chart container puts `.recharts-tooltip-wrapper` at `z-50` (a later absolute sibling, the donut number, painted above it); the back button is `w-fit` (the base `Button` is `flex`, so a ghost hover covered the row).
- Header and Visao geral: the situation badge is followed on the same line by its detail ("nunca cobrado", "5 dias restantes"). (The "Conta" and "Plano" line and the "nunca cobrado" next to the Vitalício badge were removed on 16/10/2026, see `docs/adr/2026-10-16-goal-progress-meter-and-no-account-type.md`.) Visao geral opens with six `Stat` tiles in the owner's order (situacao, plano, IA no mes with the percentage large, WhatsApp, ultima atividade, alertas) and keeps Cliente and Montagem e agente below as small lists. Every `data-slot` the specs used stayed on the same text.
- History picker (Uso de IA only): a button next to the chips opens "Este mes", "Mes passado", one item per earlier month back to the first turn of the company, and "Todo o periodo". A month is per-day columns through the existing `admin_cliente_uso` with that month's window (no new SQL); "Todo o periodo" is per-month columns through the NEW `admin_cliente_uso_meses` (migration `20261015201151`, additive, service_role only, window capped at 60 months in the function, same turn condition as the other two). The first turn comes from one indexed read (`agent_turns` order by `created_at`, limit 1) returned as `primeiraAtividade`. Route: `GET /uso?mes=AAAA-MM` and `?tudo=1` (400 on a malformed or future month). The line uses the TODAY limit and the screen says so; by month the pace is the monthly limit itself. Atendimento does not get the picker: its four periods come from the panel functions with their own window arrays and one bulk read, and a month there would need new windows in `painel_janelas`, `painel_motivos`, `painel_series` and `admin_cliente_solucoes`.
- Edit cap moved to Acoes: the same `EditarLimiteDialog` (mounted in the detail), a "Limite de IA do mes" card with the current value and an options row where per-plan presets will be added.
- Speed of the detail: (1) `app/(app)/clientes/[id]/loading.tsx` (the same pattern as `inbox/[id]/loading.tsx`): a skeleton shaped like the detail, prefetched in production; (2) the detail warms the two heavy tabs when the browser is idle (`aquecer`: their code chunks and the two aggregate reads), and the API source remembers a successful read (`lembrar`, a failure is not remembered, so "tentar de novo" still reads); (3) the five tabs are `memo` and receive stable props, so a tab switch redraws nothing else. Measured on the dev server with the owner session (same load conditions, two runs each): first open of Uso de IA 885 ms to 375 ms, of Atendimento 910 ms to 102 ms; tab switches after that are 3 to 19 ms to two animation frames. Opening a client from the list: with only the generic group skeleton the screen did not change for about 280 ms (the group `loading.tsx` never showed on this transition); with the segment skeleton the first paint is at about 150 ms in dev, and the detail is ready at about 455 ms in dev, because dev has no prefetch (Next prefetches only in production) and pays one more round for the boundary. In production the skeleton is prefetched by the visible rows, so the click shows it at once.

## Consequences
- A new screen taller than a short window must not rely on page scroll: the shell never scrolls, a card does.
- `UsoDoPeriodo.periodo` is nullable (a month or "all" is not a chip) and carries `instante`, the clock inside the month the columns belong to (the pace uses that month's days). `UsoDoCliente` gained `primeiraAtividade`; `PreviewDoCliente` carries the months as data (a Server Component cannot pass functions to the browser).
- The Uso chart for "Todo o periodo" scans the company's turns once per opening through the `(client_id, created_at)` index; at the owner's scale it is one aggregate, and the 60 month cap in SQL bounds it.
- Warming reads two aggregates per opened client even when the tabs are never used. One admin, on idle; reversible by deleting the effect in `DetalheCliente`.
- Tests: `e2e/central-clientes-ajustes.design.spec.ts` (new: pure rules and the screens above, including a short-window check that the page never scrolls), the filter spec edits in `central-clientes.design.spec.ts` and `central-clientes-acoes.design.spec.ts`, the Dia and peak map edits, and `central-clientes-lib.design.spec.ts` (Dia columns, `usoBruto`).
