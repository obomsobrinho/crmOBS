# Charts: the shadcn `chart` (Recharts) joins the base layer, and the central's Uso de IA and Atendimento tabs use it
- Date: 2026-10-15
- Status: Accepted (owner decisions of 14/10/2026 in `docs/plano-central-clientes.md`, "Delivery 1 revision"; awaiting architect review)
- Area: ui, admin, dashboard

## Context
The panel (`/painel`) draws its charts by hand (SVG and CSS) and `dashboard.md` said "no chart library". The reasons were real: the palette has ONE categorical color (the brand) plus gray, green/amber/red are state, and a library receiving colors from JS would break the cookie theme switch. The owner looked at delivery 1 of the central (`/clientes/[id]`: hand-made bars and grid) and asked for a richer dashboard: real charts, a tooltip on every one, a period filter, and variety where each type fits ("da para melhorar muito").

## Decision
- Chart library: `recharts` 3.8.0 (the version the shadcn `chart` component targets, pinned exactly in `package.json`) plus `react-is` 19.2.4 (same version as React: Recharts 3 reads element types with it, and the hoisted `react-is` 16 from `prop-types` does not know React 19 elements). npm (`package-lock.json`).
- `components/ui/chart.tsx` is the ONLY door to Recharts: `ChartContainer` (ResponsiveContainer plus one `--color-<key>` per series), `ChartTooltip` (no position animation), `ChartTooltipContent` (value strong, name secondary, a short line key instead of a box, a `titulo` and a `rodape` the screen fills), `ChartTooltipLinha`, `ChartLegenda` (an HTML list beside the chart, always present with two series or more, with values) and `useAnimacaoDoGrafico` (reduced motion shows the final drawing at once). `components/ui/mapa-de-calor.tsx` is the chart Recharts does not have (a grid with one hue in five steps, the peak outlined, a legend of the scale, one tooltip, arrow keys).
- Themed ONLY with the design-system tokens: `--chart-1` (the brand, the main series), `--chart-2` (neutral, the second series), `--chart-guia` (a reference line), `--chart-grade`, `--chart-eixo`, `--chart-cursor` and the one-hue ramp `--chart-rampa-0..4` in `app/globals.css`. They are aliases of the house tokens declared once, so they follow the theme with no dark copy. Green, amber and red stay state. The palette check (`validate_palette.js` of the dataviz skill) passes the brand and neutral pair in light and dark (CVD 25, normal 29, contrast 3:1); the neutral fails only the "chroma floor", which is the point of a neutral. A categorical set with more hues cannot be seated in this palette (brand against blue 12.8, brand against violet 8.3, both below 15), so every multi-slice chart uses the brand ramp and neutrals, with identity carried by the legend (name, number, fraction) and never by color alone.
- Every chart has a hover tooltip and the same on keyboard focus (Recharts `accessibilityLayer`; the heatmap grid is one tab stop with arrow keys), and a text view (`ChartTabela`): a table (`sr-only`, in a wrapper div: a table ignores `overflow` and made the page 3px wider than a 375px phone) with the same numbers.
- Bundle: the two chart tabs load with `dynamic()` (the Visao geral does not pay for Recharts); `mapa-de-calor.tsx` takes its balloon frame from `balao.ts`, not from `chart.tsx`. The columns of the Uso chart use the panel's own bucket skeleton (`chavesDoEsqueleto`, `lib/metrics.ts`), so panel and central draw the same hours and days.
- Charts of the central, by what the data is:
  - Uso de IA: columns of tokens per hour ("Dia") or per day (the others) with the dashed monthly-limit pace line on the same axis. Columns because the question is "was this day heavy"; the line is the cap divided by the days of the month (and by 24 for hours), pure arithmetic over the company's real cap (`ritmoDoLimite`). The cumulative month against the cap is the meter at the top of the card (the counter that decides the stop). A cumulative line on the chart was rejected: the period is a rolling window that does not start on day 1, and the month total on a second scale is a dual axis. Four tiles (tokens, turns, estimated US$, busiest column) follow the same period filter.
  - Horarios de pico: the heatmap (weekday x hour, 30 days, does not follow the filter, and says so).
  - Atendimento: stacked columns (AI and team replies over time, `barrasDasSeries` over `painel_series`, the same columns as the panel's movement), a donut (how help requests were resolved: five slices at most, with the total in the hole), horizontal bars (why the AI called the team: long names and an order), plus the four numbers.
- Period filter: the SAME chips and keys as `/painel` (`SeletorDePeriodo`, `lib/periodo.ts`: Dia, Semana, Quinzena, Mes). Both tabs bring the four periods in one read, so switching fetches nothing. Dia is the last 24 hours by hour; the others are civil days of Sao Paulo ending today (1, 7, 15, 30).
- Data: `admin_cliente_uso_horas` (new, additive, service_role only, window capped at 48 hours in the function) feeds the Dia period; the deployed `admin_cliente_uso` is untouched, so the code already in production keeps working. The Atendimento read adds one `painel_series` call (through the panel's loader, now `carregarSeriesDoPainel`, which `carregarAgregadoDoPainel` also uses), so who replied stays `lib/mensagem.ts` and there is no fourth copy of that rule in SQL.

## Consequences
- The panel's hand-made charts stay as they are (their tests and animations are tied to them); the "no chart library" line of `dashboard.md` now applies to them only. A chart in new code goes through `components/ui/chart.tsx`.
- `painel_series` has no lower bound on the date: it scans the tenant's messages like window 0 of `painel_janelas`. The Atendimento tab now costs two panel-sized reads per opening (on demand, one client at a time). A bounded variant would be a fourth copy of "who replied" in SQL; kept out on purpose, owner's call if the cost shows up.
- The Atendimento columns count civil days, with today; a 7-day panel window starts mid-day, so the sum of the columns can be slightly below "Respostas da IA". The screen says so.
- The old Uso tiles "Hoje / 7 dias / Mes" (calendar month) were replaced by tiles that follow the period chip (Mes is now the rolling 30 days). The calendar month lives in the meter (the cap counter) and the "Custo estimado do mes" line.
- Recharts renders the bars as `path.recharts-rectangle` (`.recharts-bar-rectangle` is the active-bar layer) and the axis values as `.recharts-cartesian-axis-tick-value`; tests and the axis color selector rely on that. The tooltip of a chart that is not hovered stays in the DOM without an active state: scope tooltip locators to the chart.
- Tests: `e2e/central-clientes.design.spec.ts` (period chips and sums per period, tooltip numbers against the table view, the pace line following the cap, heatmap tooltip, legend, peak and keyboard, donut and bars tooltips, empty states, light and dark colors, no horizontal overflow at 375 and 1440), `e2e/central-clientes-lib.design.spec.ts` (hours, periods, pace, movement), `e2e/central-clientes.auth.spec.ts` (API shape, read only, not run by the delivery agent).
