# Central "Clientes", second round of 16/10/2026: peak hours with a period filter, per-plan AI caps, ideal-spend bar, client model and reais (PTAX), cards with deltas
- Date: 2026-10-16
- Status: Accepted (owner decisions of 16/10/2026; awaiting architect review)
- Area: admin, ui, data, agent-ai

## Context
After the layout round of 15/10 the owner approved five more changes: the "Horários de pico" heatmap is replaced by his variation A (three tiles and two column charts) and must follow a period filter, including a custom date range; the AI cap becomes per plan (and this is REAL enforcement, not a screen); the usage meter must say where the cap comes from and show the ideal spend; the screen must show which model the client uses and costs in reais; and every card of the detail follows his reference (big number, delta against the previous period, progress to a goal, donut with a value legend, sparkline).

## Decision
- Per-plan caps, one source: `lib/custo-ia.ts`. `LIMITE_POR_PLANO` = Essencial 13 mi, Profissional 33 mi, Avançado 100 mi tokens per SP month; no plan (or an unknown plan key) keeps `LIMITE_PADRAO_TOKENS` = 10 mi. `limiteDaEmpresa(gravado, plano)`: a custom `clients.ia_limite_tokens` wins, else the plan's cap, else the default.
  - Derivation (`DERIVACAO_DO_LIMITE`, `limiteDerivadoDoPlano`): plan conversations per month (`lib/billing.ts` `PLANS`: 400 / 1.000 / 3.000) x 6 agent turns per conversation x about 4.400 tokens per turn x 1,25 margin = 13,2 mi / 33 mi / 99 mi, rounded by the owner to 13 / 33 / 100. The 6 turns and the 4.400 tokens are the owner's measurement; a check on 16/10 over the real non-bench turns of the last 30 days gave 4.542 tokens per turn (25 turns, one phone: too small to replace his number, consistent with it). A spec keeps each approved number within 1,6% of its derivation.
  - Every caller passes the plan: `processTurn` (`lib/agent-turn.ts`, the real stop; `billing_plan` joined the single `clients` select that already runs in parallel with the `consumo_ia_mensal` PK read, so no extra round trip), `estadoDaEmpresaNoMes` (`lib/consumo-ia.ts`: the app banner, `/orientar`, the notice thresholds), `paraEmpresa` and `comLimite` (`lib/admin-empresas.ts`: the list, the detail, the alerts) and the limit route (`PUT .../limite`, which reads the company's plan to compute the notice thresholds `limiaresDoLimite` it sends to `admin_mudar_limite_ia`). `admin_empresas_pagina` does not compute the cap (it returns `ia_limite_tokens` and `billing_plan`; the cap is computed in TypeScript), so no SQL changed. `EmpresaItem` gained `planoChave` and `origemDoLimite` ("Plano Avançado", "Limite personalizado", "Sem plano").
  - Effect on 16/10 (checked on the database): 5 companies, none with a stored custom cap; 4 have no plan (stay at 10 mi), OBM is Avançado (10 mi becomes 100 mi). Nobody gets a smaller cap than before. A company that changes plan changes cap at once unless it has a custom value.
  - Ações, card "Limite de IA do mês": presets Essencial 13 mi, Profissional 33 mi, Avançado 100 mi (each stores that value as a custom cap), "Padrão do plano" (stores null: follows the plan, or 10 mi without one) and "Valor personalizado" (the existing dialog). They go through `fonte.mudarLimite` into the same route and transaction as before; the screen recomputes the row with `comLimite`.
- Meter (Uso de IA and Visão geral): "2,4 mi de 100 mi tokens (Plano Avançado)". The text "Limite padrão do sistema. Custo estimado do mês... Para mudar o limite, use a aba Ações" is gone. SUPERSEDED the same day by `docs/adr/2026-10-16-goal-progress-meter-and-no-account-type.md`: the second amber bar and its legend became a goal-progress bar with a pace marker (the ideal spend so far is still cap x `fracaoDoMesSP`, SP month, UTC-3).
- Model and money. `modeloDoCliente` (`lib/admin.ts`): the `model` of the client's latest real turn (not bench, one indexed row), else the system model (`AGENT_MODEL`); the screen says which and why (`textoDoModelo`), and the estimated cost of the whole period uses that model's price (`custoEstimadoUsd`, unknown model stays null). Costs are shown in REAIS at the Banco Central PTAX of sale: `lib/cambio-servidor.ts` (server-only) calls the PTAX Olinda open data service, function `CotacaoDolarPeriodo` (`dataInicial`, `dataFinalCotacao` as `'MM-DD-AAAA'`), confirmed against the service `$metadata` and a live call on 16/10/2026 (one closing bulletin per business day: `cotacaoCompra`, `cotacaoVenda`, `dataHoraCotacao`), asks for the last 10 days and takes the newest, so weekends and holidays use the last business day. The URL carries today's date, so the 6-hour fetch cache (Next data cache) turns over daily and picks up the 13h05 bulletin. A 4-second timeout, a malformed answer or no quote gives `null`: the screen then shows US$ and says "Cotação do dólar indisponível agora", never an invented rate. The pure part (URL, parsing, `custoCurto`, `notaDoCambio`) is in `lib/cambio.ts` and `lib/custo-ia.ts`, tested without a network. The preview uses a fixed fake rate and `?semcambio=1` shows the fallback.
- Peak hours, variation A (`lib/picos.ts`, pure, built to be ported to the tenants' panel). Every filter is a window of civil days of São Paulo, inclusive (chips: the last 1, 7, 15 and 30 days with today; a civil month, cut at today; "Todo o período" from the first turn; a custom range, cut at today, up to 1830 days). `resumoDePicos` returns three tiles: Horário de pico (the 3-hour band with the most turns SUMMED over the period, "12h às 15h" and its count: the same column "Por horário do dia" highlights, so tile and chart never disagree; review of 16/10 fixed a first version that headlined a weekday + band), Dia de pico (the busiest DATE with weekday, "terça, 24/12") and Média por dia (total over the days the window covers since the first turn); and two column charts: "Por dia" (one column per DATE up to 15 days, per weekday summed above 15) and "Por horário do dia" (8 bands), the peak column in the brand color and the rest in `--chart-barra-neutra`, with tooltips, a legend and text tables. One filter drives the whole Uso tab (the four tiles, the chart, the donut and the peaks).
  - Data: the NEW `admin_cliente_picos(p_client, p_de[], p_ate[])` (migration `20261015220551`, additive, service_role only) returns, for up to 8 windows in ONE call, `{dias:[[date,turns]], cubo:[[weekday,hour,turns]]}`, in America/Sao_Paulo, same turn condition as `admin_cliente_uso`, reading `agent_turns` by the `(client_id, created_at)` index; it clamps each window to the company's first turn and to 1830 days. Always an aggregate, never rows. The 4 chips come in the base `/uso` read (one extra call, 4 windows); a month, "Todo o período" and a range come in `GET /uso?mes=|tudo=1|de=&ate=` as `{ uso, picos, janela }`. Checked against the database: for every chip window the sum of the picos equals the turns of the uso, and a month's picos equal that month's turns.
  - Custom range: a dialog from the picker ("Período personalizado…") with two dates (min = first turn, max = today), validated by `janelaDoIntervalo`; one column per day up to 62 days, per month above. The route answers 400 for malformed, inverted, future-only or over-long ranges. `picosDeHistorico` is the same aggregate in memory (preview and the definition the tests compare with). The old weekday x hour heatmap left the screen; `mapa-de-calor.tsx` stays in the base layer, unused.
- Cards (`Indicador`, `Variacao`, `ChartSparkline`): a big number, a delta badge against the previous equal period (arrow = what the number did; color = the state it means through `lib/delta.ts` `Direcao`: tokens and cost are "menor é melhor" so a rise is red, turns and replies are neutral, the first-answer time is "menor é melhor" so a fall is green; with no base the badge becomes a sentence, never "+100%"; cost deltas always in percent), a sparkline of the same columns as the big chart where there is a series, a progress bar where there is a goal (the cap meter, attendants against the plan's seats in Financeiro) and a donut with a value legend where there is a part of a whole (new: "De onde vêm os tokens" = new input, cached input, output; the resolutions donut already existed). Previous-period bases come from the SAME aggregates, never invented: the chips compare with the N days before (`admin_cliente_uso` now reads 61 days, `admin_cliente_uso_horas` 48 hours, the function's own cap) and the Dia compares today so far with yesterday up to the same hour; a month with the previous month; a range with the equal range before it; "Todo o período" has none. Atendimento: replies of the AI and first-answer time get a delta from a second `painel_janelas` call over the panel's own previous windows (`limites`: the equal window, and for the Dia the same weekday last week). The conversations metric (tiles "Conversas" and "Só a IA atendeu") is left alone and has no delta: its definition is pending with the owner. Financeiro opens with four number cards (situação, plano with its price, atendentes against the plan's seats with a bar, cobrança) and keeps the date list and provider cards below.

## Review round (16/10/2026, after the code review)
- The cap follows the plan, so the notice marks must follow too: `admin_mudar_limite_ia` was the only place that cleared the 80%/100% marks, and a plan change (`admin_mudar_conta` `mudar_plano`, `POST /api/billing/subscribe`) could leave a mark that suppressed a notice at the new cap. New `limpar_marcas_consumo_ia` (migration `20261016170702`, additive, service_role only) with the thresholds of `limiaresDaEmpresa` (`lib/custo-ia.ts`, the SAME rule as editing the limit), called by `alinharMarcasDoMes` (`lib/consumo-ia.ts`, best effort, never throws) after the plan write. The Asaas webhook never writes `billing_plan`, so it needs nothing.
- Presets in Ações: "Padrão do plano" clears the custom limit (null) so the cap keeps following the plan; the number of ANOTHER plan only opens the "Valor personalizado" box with it prefilled. Storing a plan's number as a custom limit silently stopped the company from following its plan. Labels come from `limiteDaEmpresa(null, plano)`.
- The pace is the pace of the month each COLUMN belongs to (`ritmoDaBarra`, `trechosDoRitmo`): a 30-day chip that crosses two months draws one dashed segment per month. `posicaoNoRitmo` is the one "at or below the pace" rule (meter, sentence, projection). The `T03:00` start-of-day literals became `inicioDoDiaSP` (`lib/fuso.ts`).
- Deltas use the panel's `Selo` over `lib/delta.ts` (`calcularDelta` got `piso`; `piso: 0` means always a percentage, for tokens and cost) and the first-answer time uses `deltaDuracao` (seconds or minutes, never a percentage). `IndicadorComparado` wires the delta, the "comparado com" legend and the sparkline once (kept out of `pecas.tsx` because it pulls Recharts). The cost sparkline tooltip now writes the currency (`custoDoPonto`).
- Efficiency: `atendimentoDoCliente` reads the four current and four previous windows in ONE `painel_janelas` call; the model of the client is read in parallel with the usage (and bounded to the last 61 days, falling back to the whole history only when there is none); the Uso/Atendimento DATA is read only when the owner points at the tab (the code chunks still warm on idle). `admin_cliente_picos` still scans four nested windows: its weekday x hour cube has no date dimension, so a shorter window cannot be derived from the 30-day one without a new per-date-and-hour function; left as is on purpose.
- The PTAX no longer blocks the page: the server hands the promise to the screen (`useCambio`), which opens in US$ and swaps to reais; timeout 2 s, a failure is remembered for 10 minutes. The PTAX types and formatters moved to `lib/cambio.ts`, the donut font helper to `lib/admin-cliente.ts`; `lib/custo-ia.ts` is prices, caps and consumption again.
- Cleanup: `mapa-de-calor.tsx` and `--chart-rampa-tinta*` deleted; `lib/admin-cliente.ts` lost the dead `cubo`/`cuboDe` path (the SQL keeps its parameter), `janelaDoMes`, the weekday tables and `rotuloDoDia` (`textoDoDia` of `lib/picos.ts`); the four `SelecaoDeUso` switches became `descricaoDaSelecao`; server and preview share `leituraDoHistorico`/`usoDoHistorico`; the goal bar is `MedidorDeMeta`; the check-mark menu item is `DropdownMenuItemMarcado`. The track and `neutro` colour change of `Medidor` was the owner's request (visible tracks, 15/10) and stays; the `z-50` on the chart tooltip only touches the central charts (they are the only users of `ChartContainer`).

## Consequences
- The cap is now a function of the plan: changing a company's plan changes its cap and the app banner, the notices and the `processTurn` stop follow at the next turn. A custom value stays until someone sets "Padrão do plano".
- The cost shown is an estimate: tokens of the period at the price table of the latest model of the client, in reais at the PTAX of the last business day (not the rate of each charge). The screen says so.
- Every Uso opening runs one more aggregate (`admin_cliente_picos`, 4 windows, bounded by the first turn) and the detail's warm-up reads it with the rest; "Todo o período" and long ranges scan the client's turns once, bounded at 1830 days.
- The picos rule is the one to port to the tenants' panel: only `lib/picos.ts` and the function need to move; the central's screens consume it.
- The OBM counter (`consumo_ia_mensal`, 2,4 mi) and the registered turns of the month (14,5 mi, mostly bench) differ because the counter started on 13/10; the screen already says the charts sum the registered turns.
- Tests: `e2e/central-clientes-ajustes.design.spec.ts` (windows, resumo, plan caps and derivation, PTAX URL and parsing, deltas, the screens above), the edits of `central-clientes.design.spec.ts` and `central-clientes-lib.design.spec.ts`, and `central-clientes.auth.spec.ts` for the API shape and the picos against the database (written and not run in this round: login suites are the orchestrator's).
