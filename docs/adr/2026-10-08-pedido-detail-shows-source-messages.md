# The help request detail shows the source messages in sections; the AI summary is a labeled title
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Status: Accepted
- Area: pedidos, data, realtime

## Context
The `/pedidos` sheet showed "O que foi pedido" as the AI summary (`handoffs.summary`). The summary is the model's paraphrase, and the owner had to open the conversation to see what the client actually wrote, what the AI answered and what happened after the orientation. A paraphrase can drift from what was said (case M8 of the attendance battery: the summary said an agreed conversation had not happened). The list column was also as wide as the sheet while showing only a name and one line.

A first version the same day drew a timeline. The owner rejected it: on a request with no client message it showed an empty "Pedido aberto" row whose reason repeated the facts strip, then a resolution row that repeated it again. His words: on top the summary, below the message that originated the request, "não precisa ser linha do tempo, somente os cards/sessões com o que aconteceu".

## Decision
- The detail shows section cards in ONE vertical column, in reading order, at the full width of the sheet on every screen size (the owner rejected a side-by-side version: a resolved request showed an empty client card on the left and the orientation on the right). Never a timeline and never chat bubbles (`lib/historia-do-pedido.ts` `montarSecoes`, pure; `components/SecoesDoPedido.tsx`; `Card variant="secao"`, a block inside the page card). Only the cards with content, in this order, and on an open request the "Orientar a IA" box last:
  - "O que o cliente escreveu": the client messages that led to the request, verbatim, each with its time, as quotes: up to 5 from the 12 hours before `opened_at`; media shows its type, an audio shows the transcription n8n stored in `user_message`. Below a small "depois do pedido" divider, the same card lists what the client sent WHILE the request waited (between `opened_at` and `closed_at`, or now when open), in order, each with its time: the 10 newest of that range, with "Há mensagens anteriores a estas: veja na conversa." when there were more. The database limit counts client rows only (`FILTRO_DO_CLIENTE`, the PostgREST form of `linhaDoCliente`), so team media in the period cannot hide the "more messages" hint. With nothing before and nothing during, one neutral line inside the card ("Nenhuma mensagem do cliente encontrada antes do pedido.").
  - "Orientação do time", when there is one.
  - "Resposta da IA" and/or "Resposta do time": the REPLIES sent afterwards, grouped by who sent it per `lib/mensagem.ts` (`respostaDaIa`, `respostaHumana`); only reply rows are read, so client messages never use up the limit (code review 2026-10-13: the first 6 rows of any kind hid a team reply sent 2 hours later). Resolved request: up to 6 replies from `closed_at` minus 2 minutes, within 24 hours. OPEN request: nobody answered it yet, so there is no "Resposta da IA" (the AI's own sentence when opening, "vou confirmar com o time", is not an answer); only "Resposta do time" when the team sent a manual message while it is open, up to 6 from the opening. AI replies split on `" | "` like the conversation. Client messages after the CLOSING are not shown (the conversation has them).
- Reason, opening and closing times and the resolution live ONLY in the facts strip at the top; no section repeats them.
- The AI summary sits on top, labeled "Resumo da IA", so it reads as the model's interpretation. It stays the row title in the list and the WhatsApp notice text, unchanged.
- Loading: only when a request is opened, never in the list query. `FontePedidos.historia` runs three small queries on `chat_messages` (before, during, after) (explicit columns, `client_id` + `phone` + time window + `limit`, served by `chat_messages_client_phone_created_idx`). The memory source of `/design` applies the same windows in code (`recorteAntes`, `recorteDepois`). The notices number is never queried (`ehNumeroDeAvisos`).
- Realtime: while the request is open or the "after" window is still open, `useCanalConversa` (`chat_messages` filtered by the phone on the server, `modo: "aplicar"`) inserts the ONE row from the payload (`encaixarNaHistoria`: a client message in the waiting period joins "during", keeping the 10 newest; a REPLY in the after window joins "after"), zero queries; reconnecting or returning focus rereads only this request's two queries. Old requests do not subscribe.
- Layout: the list is a narrow column (340px, 380px from `xl`) and the sheet takes the rest. The sheet body is one column on every screen (owner, 2026-10-08). The reason filter sits next to the search, compact ("Motivo" until a reason is chosen), so the search placeholder reads whole at 340px.

## Consequences
- Opening a request costs two bounded queries; the list cost is unchanged.
- The windows (12h before, waiting period, 24h after; 5, 10 and 6 rows) live in `lib/historia-do-pedido.ts`; the database query and the memory source must keep using them.
- Proof: `e2e/pedidos.design.spec.ts` (pure windows and grouping, verbatim messages, empty client card, media label, orientation and AI reply side by side, "Time assumiu" with the team reply, no timeline markup, layout widths, filter position and placeholder fit at 340px); `e2e/pedidos.serial.spec.ts` (the client card loads against the real database).
